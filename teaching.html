Instructional and computational resources

These repositories are independent educational extensions developed from graduate coursework. They are not presented as courses taught at the University of Delaware and do not redistribute restricted course materials or answer keys.

  • Environmental Economics Lab — lecture modules paired with real-data demonstrations.
  • Resource Economics Lab — dynamic models of scarcity, extraction, fisheries, forestry, water, and sustainability.
  • Microeconomics Lab — computational demonstrations of microeconomic theory.
  • enviroML — applied machine learning for environmental data.
