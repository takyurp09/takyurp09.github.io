Dissertation research

Combining empirical and process-based crop models for climate-impact estimation

Dissertation chapter · Work in progress

Combines an empirical fixed-effects yield model with three process-based crop models for maize, rice, and wheat; evaluates a constrained ensemble with leave-one-region-out validation; and studies projections under two SSP scenarios.
