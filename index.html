Muhammad Taky Tahmid

PhD Candidate in Environmental Economics, University of Delaware

I am a PhD candidate in Environmental Economics at the University of Delaware. My research examines how climate change affects agriculture, food security, production, and economic outcomes.

I combine applied econometrics with climate-model output, satellite observations, process-based crop models, and large geospatial datasets. My dissertation work includes hybrid modeling for improved crop-yield estimation.

Across this work, I focus on transparent measurement, careful model validation, and policy-relevant interpretation.
