Environmental economist

Muhammad Taky Tahmid

PhD Candidate in Environmental Economics · University of Delaware

I study how climate change shapes agriculture, food security, production, and economic outcomes.

My research combines applied econometrics with climate-model output, satellite observations, process-based crop models, and large geospatial datasets.

Across this work, I focus on transparent measurement, careful model validation, and policy-relevant interpretation.

Current research

Hybrid modeling for improved crop yield estimation

My first dissertation chapter combines an empirical fixed-effects yield model with process-based crop models for maize, rice, and wheat. The constrained ensemble is evaluated with leave-one-region-out validation and applied to yield projections under SSP scenarios.
